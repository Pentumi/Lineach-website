Semiconductor Tool Hook-Up & High-Purity Utility Connections

Precision connections for advanced manufacturing facilities across Ireland.

Lineach delivers precision tool hook-up and high-purity process piping for semiconductor and advanced manufacturing facilities across Ireland. We specialise in the final, critical connections between process tools and facility utilities — process gases, ultra-pure water, chemicals, vacuum, CDA, and exhaust — executed to the highest cleanliness and documentation standards.

What We Deliver

  • Tool hook-up of process gases, UPW/DI water, chemicals, vacuum, and process cooling water
  • High-purity and ultra-high-purity stainless steel and specialised polymer systems
  • Orbital welding with monitored inert gas purging
  • Cleanroom-compatible installation and gowning protocols
  • Full material traceability, leak testing, and documentation packages
  • Coordination with tool OEMs and main contractors during installation and start-up

Why Lineach

  • Dual-certified welders (ASME IX & EN287)
  • Experience in regulated, high-spec environments
  • In-house fabrication and spooling capability for faster, cleaner installation
  • Strong focus on contamination control, schedule reliability, and complete handover documentation
